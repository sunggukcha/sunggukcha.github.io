Summary

I am a researcher in the Service AI Research Team at LG Uplus. My work spans information retrieval, multimodal learning, document intelligence, and agentic systems. Previously, I developed multimodal and computer-vision systems at NCSOFT and MINDs Lab. I received my M.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from UNIST.
